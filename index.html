Mission & Vision

Mission

To give the faintest sparks what they need most: time, capital and conviction.

Vision

A world with no ceiling on what is possible, where technology widens the frontier, products renew everyday life, and one person is enough to build something great.

Principles

  1. 01

    Before consensus

    The best opportunities appear before they have a name. When the data is thin, we decide on research and judgment.

  2. 02

    Here for the long run

    We think in decades. We take no credit in good times and keep our seat in hard ones.

  3. 03

    Founders first

    A company belongs to the people who build it. We bring resources and perspective, and leave the wheel with the founder.

  4. 04

    Independent judgment

    No chasing heat, no faith in labels. Every investment is researched and owned by a partner.

Investment Focus

Our allocation
is our conviction

The portfolio is built 70 / 20 / 10. Its weight sits in frontier technology, with room kept for the products and brands that change how people live, and for individuals who create on their own.

Areas of interest

  • AI & autonomous agents
  • Embodied intelligence & robotics
  • Compute & semiconductors
  • Energy & advanced materials
  • Life sciences & synthetic biology
  • Space technology

What we look for

Teams with a defensible technical edge and a chance to define a new category. We are willing to come in before the product takes shape and stay through long R&D cycles.

Areas of interest

  • AI-native consumer products
  • Smart hardware
  • Health & longevity
  • Next-generation brands
  • Culture & experiences

What we look for

Brands driven by product strength that can travel across cycles and borders. Great consumer companies are where new technology becomes everyday life.

Areas of interest

  • Indie developers
  • Designers & artists
  • Content & knowledge creators
  • Open-source authors
  • Small studios

What we look for

The work comes before the company. We back creators with a distinct voice and an audience already listening, with flexible structures that follow the creator’s own pace.
